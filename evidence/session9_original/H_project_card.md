2. **Predictive vs. Causal Terminology Correction:** Earlier slides/docs used the shorthand `'Causal ML'` to mean *strictly chronological past-only priors ($\tau < t$) without future data leakage*. Because Random Forest/GBT performs observational forecasting rather than interventional Do-calculus (`do(X)` with confounder adjustment), we explicitly rename it **`Predictive ML with Past-Only Temporal Priors (\tau < t)`**.
3. **Sparse GPS Probes (`11.85%` observed vs `88.15%` zero-observation):** Only `10,027 / 84,633` segments have GPS pings; `74,606` (`88.15%`) have `0` pings (`Cold-Start`). A zero-observation segment means *no probe vehicle reported on that edge during the sample window*, NOT that the road has zero traffic. Structural `PageRank` alone is never displayed as live congestion.
4. **Graph Validity (`Line-Graph PageRank` vs Observed Speed/LOS):** Tested in Mini-Test `B2`: `segment_id = 21969` (`Nguyễn Kiệm`, `primary`, `PageRank = 2.7313`, `in=3, out=1`) averages **`45.41 km/h` (`LOS A`)** across `24` windows because its wide multi-lane carriageway absorbs merging inflows, whereas `segment_id = 34019` (`Trần Quốc Thảo`, `PageRank = 1.2818`) averages **`7.25 km/h` (`LOS F`)**. Thus `PageRank` measures *topological funneling vulnerability*, which only turns into congestion when combined with capacity & temporal demand.
5. **Spatial K-Means (`7D Behavioral & Morphological Clusters`):** Tested in Mini-Test `B2`: because `7D K-Means` clusters on `[length_m, street_level, max_velocity, in_degree, out_degree, pagerank_score, street_type_code]` without lat/lon coordinates, roads in the same cluster (e.g., `Cluster 1`: `Hà Huy Giáp` in Q.12, `Lý Thường Kiệt` in Q.10, `Quốc lộ 13` in Thủ Đức) are geographically non-contiguous. We explicitly call them **`7D Behavioral & Morphological Road Archetypes`**.
6. **Streaming / Sketch Accuracy (`Bloom 0.616% FPR`, `HLL++ 3.98% Error`):** Tested in Mini-Test `B3`: `HLL++ (1.0 KiB)` estimates `9,628` vs `10,027` exact active segments (`3.98%` relative error) and only feeds the global coverage KPI banner on Tab 1 (`0` impact on road alerts). `Bloom Filter (32.0 KiB)` catches `334/334` injected duplicates (`0.616%` FPR), and nightly Tier-2 Batch Reconciliation restores `100.0%` (`33,441/33,441`) completeness.
7. **Batch–Speed Reconciliation & Audit Versions:** Every serving document in `MongoDB` carries `lifecycle_status` (`STREAM_PROVISIONAL` `v1` at online issuance vs `RECONCILED_COMPLETE` `v2` after nightly late-arrival backfill), `status_version` (`1` vs `2`), and `previous_lifecycle_status`, ensuring `v2` never overwrites the audit trail of what was issued at time `t` and stale `v1` replays (`400` tested) are deterministically rejected.

## 5. One-Minute Defense Answer
> **Question:** *What does the system know at the exact forecast time on road segments with no GPS observations?*
> **Evidence-Based Answer (60s):** At forecast origin $t$ on any of the `74,606` (`88.15%`) zero-GPS segments, the system has **zero live vehicle velocity pings** and never fabricates synthetic sensor readings. Instead, it knows three strictly pre-computed, leak-free structural signals from the Phase A `Static Feature Store` (`data/lakehouse/segment_static_features.parquet`): **(1) Road geometry & hierarchy** (`length_m`, `street_level`, `street_type`, and Tier-2/Tier-3 `max_velocity` fitted on `TRAIN`), **(2) Topological funneling centrality** (`Line-Graph PageRank`, `in_degree`, `out_degree` on the pruned `52,425`-node intersection graph), and **(3) Behavioral cluster & street-level priors** (`cluster_id` from `7D K-Means` and `street_causal_prior_sev` fitted strictly on `TRAIN` $\tau < t$), combined with the exact deterministic ICT time-of-day harmonics (`period_sin`, `period_cos`, `weekday`). On the Streamlit UI (`Tab 4`), these predictions are explicitly badged as **`[COLD-START STRUCTURAL PRIOR]`** rather than live sensor telemetry.
